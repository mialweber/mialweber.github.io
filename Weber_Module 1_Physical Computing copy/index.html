Project 1 Concept

Project 1 Concept

HMI Statement: How might I create a plant care product that promotes health wellness, is affordable and aligns with future market trends?

I will be taking the design path to create a product for plant owners who want the air purifying benifits but struggle with keeping their plants healthy and happy. The product is a plant pot with built in sensors that notify the individual if the plant requires watering(details will be given for how much water and how to water) or different light conditions. The sensors will connect with the Arduino board and then my code, using an API to determine the plant's needs and then send a notification to the user's phone. The app will request the pot's product number to connect and then the plant type will be entered in order for the API to retrieve the care requirements and to check if it should display a warning if the plant can be harmful to pets.
